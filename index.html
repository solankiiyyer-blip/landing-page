Stop 11 — Questions

Asked and
answered.

What configurations are on offer?

1 BHK homes of 464–486 sq.ft carpet and 2 BHK homes of 626–642 sq.ft carpet. There is also a Jodi — two adjacent homes combined into a single residence of 1098–1105 sq.ft.

What do they cost?

Indicatively, 1 BHK from ₹1.18–1.23 Cr and 2 BHK from ₹1.60–1.64 Cr. Jodi pricing is on request. These figures exclude stamp duty, registration, GST and other statutory charges.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. The MahaRERA registration number is PM1180002502396, and you can verify it yourself at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.

When is possession?

March 2029. The project is pre-launch and currently under construction.

Where exactly is it?

Mogra Village, near Andheri Subway, on New Nagardas Road, next to Laxmi Hotel — Andheri East, Mumbai 400069.

How well connected is it?

Nine points sit five to fifteen minutes away: Andheri Railway Station, Andheri Metro Station, Mogra Metro Station, S.V. Road, the Western Express Highway, JVLR, Nagardas Road, the Airport and Jogeshwari Railway Station.

What amenities are included?

Twelve: gym, kids area, CCTV, indoor games, rainwater harvesting, gated community, security systems, lift, rooftop garden, EV charging point, fire safety system and Vastu compliant planning.

How do I see the site?

Ask Hitendra B. Solanki, the authorised channel partner for this project. Call or message +91 88281 61678 and he will arrange it. Site visits are free and there is zero brokerage.
